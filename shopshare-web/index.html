Questions

Good to know

Do I need a car to use ShopShare?

Not at all. Most people on ShopShare are riders, not hosts — you only need a car if you want to post a trip and offer seats to others.

How much does a ride cost?

Hosts set their own price per seat, usually just enough to cover gas. You'll always see the exact price before you request to join.

Is my payment information safe?

Payments are processed through Stripe, the same infrastructure used by major marketplaces. ShopShare never stores your card details.

What if my host cancels last minute?

You'll get an instant notification if a ride is cancelled, and any payment already made is refunded automatically.
